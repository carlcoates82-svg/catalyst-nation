A note from Sarah

When I started OBH Software I was usually the only woman in the room, and often the youngest. Most of the women I'd trained and worked with had the skills to lead a company. What they didn't have was a first customer, a product and someone backing them.

So I'm not waiting for the pipeline to fix itself. SJ Venture Studio does the risky early work, the discovery, the validation and the first build, on the same engineering team and AI stack that runs OBH. Every venture we prove gets a woman at the top. Every venture we build trains more women engineers on the way.

I make the call at every gate, I chair every board in year one, and I answer the phone when a CEO needs me.

The SJ Protocol

Eight stages. Sarah makes the call at every gate.

Each stage has a budget and a kill test. Agents gather the evidence and a gate pack lands on Sarah's desk. She decides whether a venture moves on or stops. The target is first paying customers inside 90 days.

  1. 01Discover

    Find expensive, repetitive work with a clear budget owner.

    Sarah picks the problems worth chasing, often from OBH's own customers.Sarah's gate: named buyer
  2. 02Validate

    Twenty buyer interviews. Confirm pain and willingness to pay.

    Sarah joins the last five interviews herself.Sarah's gate: 3 letters of intent
  3. 03Design

    Shape the offer and pricing before heavy build.

    Sarah signs off pricing using the OBH SaaS playbook.Sarah's gate: priced proposal
  4. 04Build

    MVP on the shared engine and agent stack.

    Sarah staffs the squad, with placement engineers on every build.Sarah's gate: pilot-ready
  5. 05Pilot

    Paid pilots with live customers. The CEO candidate joins here.

    Sarah chooses the Founder CEO from the bench.Sarah's gate: 3 paid pilots
  6. 06Prove

    Show the result repeats and the unit economics hold.

    Sarah and the CEO review the numbers together, weekly.Sarah's gate: repeatable sale
  7. 07Launch NewCo

    Incorporate, seat the board, install the Founder CEO.

    Sarah takes the chair and hands over operating control.Sarah's gate: board approval
  8. 08Scale

    Growth with studio governance and portfolio support.

    Sarah steps back from the chair after year one, if the CEO wants.Gate: quarterly review
Structure

One studio engine. A ring-fenced company for every venture.

Sarah's HoldCo owns the engine: engineering from OBH Software, AI systems, go-to-market and governance. Each venture that passes her gates becomes its own NewCo with its own P&L, board and Founder CEO.

Where Sarah builds

Female-focused verticals, built agent-first

Markets where women are the end user, the buyer or most of the workforce, and where the product is often built without them in mind. Each venture ships as a B2B product with a team of agents doing the admin, so the people involved get time back for care, customers and judgement.
